How it works

AI proposes. Engine computes. Dispatcher decides.
Claude (Anthropic)

Understands

  • Reads a 112 call or a crew's sentence in Slovenian
  • Triages severity and condition, says why
  • Reviews every critical and serious case
  • Proposes plans and explains the trade-off
Rules engine

Computes

  • Dispatches every call the moment it arrives
  • Simulates each option on real roads and traffic
  • Re-checks any change before it is applied
  • Every number on screen comes from here
Dispatcher

Decides

  • Assisted (default) or Autonomous mode
  • Overrides any unit, and the override sticks
  • Approves a plan with one key
  • Every decision logged: engine, AI or human

Built for the real world

Real roads, Slovenian doctrine, never empty-handed

Real roads and traffic

OSRM routes, live TomTom traffic and incidents, detours around closures, weather from two providers.

Slovenian EMS doctrine, as a model

Four severity levels with targets (critical 8 min in the city, 15 outside; serious 18; urgent 60), 14 conditions weighted by time sensitivity, doctor cars, and the HNMP helicopter as it really flies: Brnik, 13 min to take off, 08:00–16:30.

Every input has a backup

Claude falls back to the engine's plan, AI triage to keyword rules, live traffic to a time-of-day model. A 60 s deadline and a usage guard cap the AI.

For the people in the field

Crew phone with hold-to-talk in Slovenian, an in-vehicle tablet with turn-by-turn navigation, a Slovenian and English interface, and a demo lock on every control action.
