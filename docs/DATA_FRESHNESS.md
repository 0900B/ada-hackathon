# Data freshness: what is current as of 21 Nov 2026, and what changed

A second pass to find the **newest published version** of every key number and rule. For each item: what I used, the newest source I could find, and whether anything changed. Companion to [GAPS_STATUS.md](GAPS_STATUS.md) and [OPERATIONS_DETAILS.md](OPERATIONS_DETAILS.md).

**Tags:** **[S]** read in the original; **[SS]** only seen in a news report or search summary; **[I]** my inference.

## 1. The biggest change: a new emergency-service rulebook since 3 June 2026

A new **Pravilnik o službi nujne medicinske pomoči in prevozih pacientov** (Uradni list RS 666/2026) was published on **19 May 2026** and applies from **3 June 2026**. It replaced the 2015 rulebook after 11 years and merged emergency service and patient transport into one text. I could **not read the 2026 text itself**: the official gazette site has a broken security certificate, the legal-database page loads its text by script, and the news agency page blocked me. What follows comes from the Ministry's own statement and a detailed Žurnal24 article from June 2026 **[SS]**.

| Topic | Before (2015 rulebook) | Now (2026 rulebook) |
|---|---|---|
| Who responds | Out-of-area help was the exception | Dispatch assigns by the **shortest access time regardless of the provider's area**; dislocated units (Ivančna Gorica, Vrhnika and **now Medvode**) reduce gaps |
| Response standard | not a hard average | **Average access time of the first team to red (priority I) cases must not exceed 15 minutes over a year**; **exit time normally 1 minute**; a doctor vehicle may leave later, **up to 5 minutes**, with the delay documented |
| Paperwork | separate forms per team (protocol of emergency intervention, resuscitation protocol, transport report and so on) | **One digital record per event (EDSZ)**: every team and doctor on the same case writes into it |
| Where patient data go | not specified | **Central health record, not the dispatch system**; available to the receiving hospital's system **at the latest at handover** |
| Digital system | none | **IS NMP**, about **€6.36 million** (EU recovery funds), free to providers; units supply their own tablets. In testing in June 2026; **Gorenjska units first in autumn 2026, all other units in 2027**; **paper forms stay as the fallback** |
| Private and non-urgent providers | could be asked to help | Public-network non-urgent providers **must answer the dispatcher's call and join emergency operations during a sudden short-term rise in demand**; a non-urgent ambulance provider needs a Ministry permit, staff for **two teams**, **two ambulances plus a replacement**; a sanitary-transport provider needs one trained driver, one sanitary vehicle and one spare; providers outside the emergency network must mark vehicles "Prevoz pacientov" |
| Vehicles | EN 1789 types | **New standards apply to newly ordered vehicles only**; vehicles already in use need no change |
| Staff | refresher rules | **160 hours of initial training** (92 with a mentor), knowledge checks about **every seven years**, safe-driving course before starting, mandatory **psychosocial support** |
| First responders | not regulated in detail | Activated through dispatch; refresh at least yearly; until the technology exists, the regional 112 centre activates them on the dispatcher's request |
| Events with crowds | older rules | Events graded by size and risk; a very demanding event more than **30 minutes of urgent driving** from the nearest urgent centre needs a **helicopter landing site** |
| Reporting | every 6 months | every 6 months, now including **exit and arrival times and kilometres driven**, automatically where possible |
| Network of providers | inside the rulebook | moved to a government decree; the Ministry counts **61 emergency-service providers** |

**What this does to my earlier documents [I]:**
- The **form names and the 20 percent spare-vehicle rule** I quoted were from the **2015** text and may have changed. I marked them as old.
- **Ljubljana is probably still on paper or older systems in 2026**, because the digital record starts in Gorenjska in autumn and reaches everyone in 2027.
- The 15-minute handover limit, the status chain and the 2-hour replacement-vehicle rule come from the **2017 dispatch rulebook**, which a 2025 law (ZZDej-N, Uradni list RS 32/2025) amended. I did **not** find what that amendment changed, so those figures are **2017 values, not re-verified**.

## 3. Corrections made in the other documents (partly done)

- **Mechanical workshop:** removed the claim that the station has none. It has a renovated workshop, and external repairs take 12 to 96 hours on average. (The 2025 report said a *planned new building* would lack one.)
- **Paperwork:** marked the 2015 form list as replaced on 3 June 2026 and added the new digital record and its rollout.
- **Spare vehicles:** marked the 20 percent rule as 2015, not re-verified under the 2026 text.
- **Private providers:** the 2026 duty to answer dispatch calls is recorded in section 1 only; **not yet copied** into the surge document.
- **Dispatch rule and response standards:** recorded in section 1 of this file only; **not yet copied** into the surge and scenario documents.
