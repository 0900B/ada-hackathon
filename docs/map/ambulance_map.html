How to read it. A team of 1.0 is one vehicle crewed round the clock; 1.5 is crewed part of the day as well. Staff are estimates: 10.3 clinical staff per ambulance team and 11.5 per doctor-vehicle team, derived from the insurer's planning hours. For UKC Ljubljana the figure is the real 123 clinical staff (the estimate gives 123.4, which supports the formula). Vehicle counts here are vehicles on duty; the UKC station's whole fleet was 30 in 2022. Private non-urgent ambulance firms are not on this map.
